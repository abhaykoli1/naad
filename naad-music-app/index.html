The flow

Eight screens, one mood.

Onboarding → mood check → discover → now playing. Everything leans on hairlines, serif headlines and a single ivory accent.

Design system

Quiet parts, working hard.

Ivory buttons instead of brand-green. Hairline borders instead of shadows. Serif headlines in place of all-caps labels.

Direction

Familiar, not copied.

Same listening loop as the big players — different surface, language and hierarchy.

01

Mood before genre

Onboarding asks how you feel, not what you like. Home greets you with feelings, then artists.

02

Ivory, not green

Primary actions are warm off-white on charcoal. Brand colour appears only inside cover art.

03

Editorial type

Serif headlines with tight tracking give an album-sleeve feel; the UI itself stays near-invisible.

04

Generative covers

Every playlist gets a gradient-and-grain artwork generated from its mood — no stock photography.
