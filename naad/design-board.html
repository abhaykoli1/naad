Direction

Familiar, not copied.

The same listening loop as the big players — a completely different surface and language.

01

Mood before genre

Onboarding asks how you feel, not what you like. Home opens with a feeling, then artists.

02

Photography only

Every cover is a quiet photograph — dunes, water, concrete, a lit window. No gradients, no 3D blobs.

03

Glass, not chrome

Nav bar, mini player and lyrics sheet are frosted layers. The docked bar is near-transparent — content blurs and tints through it as you scroll.

04

One-screen listening

Player collapses to a single glass strip. The library never disappears behind chrome.

The USP

Moments — a diary of listening.

No mainstream music app keeps a memory of how you listened. NAAD does it on-device.

01 · Moments capture

What it records: the track, the time, how many repeats, and a one-line scene — “you played this three times walking home; it rained for eleven minutes.”
Where it lives: a card on Discover and inside Profile. One tap replays the exact moment as a playlist.
Privacy by design: processed on-device, never uploaded, and a single switch turns capture off. No social feed, no sharing — this is your memory, not content.

02 · Lyrics that translate

Time-synced: lines light up as the song plays; tap any line to jump there. The collapsed state always shows what is playing now and what comes next.
हिंदी in place: one toggle adds a Devanagari translation under every line, using Noto Sans Devanagari — built for an Indian-first audience, not an afterthought.
Bigger text: a type-size control inside the sheet for accessibility.
